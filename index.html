The prompt formula

Great prompts have four ingredients

Use these four elements to tell Copilot what you need, why it matters, what the result should look like, and which information should inform the response.

G

Goal

What outcome are you trying to achieve?

  • Prepare for a meeting
  • Analyze information
  • Create a briefing
C

Context

Why does the request matter?

  • Audience
  • Situation
  • Business purpose
E

Expectations

What should the response look like?

  • Executive briefing
  • Memo or table
  • Talking points
S

Sources

What information should Copilot use?

  • Meetings and emails
  • Chats and files
  • Credible web sources

Basic prompt

Summarize this meeting.

Stronger prompt

Act as Chief of Staff.

Review the meeting and create an executive summary that includes:

• Key decisions
• Risks and concerns
• Open issues
• Follow-up actions

Present the response as a one-page executive briefing and include supporting evidence when available.
